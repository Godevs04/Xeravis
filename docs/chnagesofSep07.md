# SECTION 2 — ABOUT XELARVIS

##

---

# 1. HERO

### Recommended headline

# **Engineering Intelligence for a Data-Driven World**

### Supporting text

> XELARVIS is an AI, data and technology company focused on helping organizations transform complex data and technology challenges into scalable, measurable solutions.

Then:

**Explore Our Capabilities →**

**Work With Us →**

### Avoid

Don't make the hero:

> "We are a leading global technology company..."

unless you can substantiate "leading" and "global."

Professional websites increasingly benefit from **specific positioning rather than oversized claims**.

---

# 2. WHO WE ARE

This should be the first substantial section.

## **Who We Are**

Suggested positioning:

> XELARVIS brings together artificial intelligence, data science, data engineering and technology consulting to help organizations build smarter, more scalable and data-driven operations.
>
> Our work spans AI solutions, advanced analytics, modern data platforms, digital transformation and healthcare & clinical data science.

Then three small pillars:

### AI & Intelligence

Building intelligent systems that turn data into actionable insight.

### Data & Analytics

Creating trusted data foundations and advanced analytical capabilities.

### Technology & Transformation

Modernizing technology environments and translating strategy into execution.

---

# 3. DON'T REPEAT ALL FIVE SERVICES HERE

This is important.

Your Services page already explains:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science & Advanced Analytics
- IT Consulting & Digital Transformation
- Data Engineering & Cloud
- Healthcare & Clinical Data Science

The About page should **introduce the philosophy behind these capabilities**, then link to Services.

Use:

> **Explore What We Do →**

instead of creating five long service descriptions.

---

# 4. OUR MISSION

This needs to be concise.

## **Our Mission**

> **To make advanced technology practical, intelligent and measurable for organizations solving complex real-world problems.**

Then explain:

> We combine research, engineering and domain expertise to create solutions that move beyond experimentation and deliver meaningful business outcomes.

This connects nicely with your existing:

**Research → Technology → Business Outcome**

positioning.

---

# 5. OUR VISION

## **Our Vision**

> **To build a technology ecosystem where intelligence, data and innovation create measurable progress for organizations and society.**

Keep the Vision short.

Don't write a 200-word vision statement.

---

# 6. WHAT MAKES XELARVIS DIFFERENT

This is probably the **most important About section**.

Don't say:

> Innovation
>
> Excellence
>
> Integrity
>
> Passion

Every technology company says these.

Instead use differentiators that relate directly to your business model.

### Research-to-Production

> We connect experimentation and research with practical technology implementation.

### Outcome-Focused

> We focus on measurable business and operational outcomes rather than technology for its own sake.

### Cross-Disciplinary

> AI, data engineering, analytics and technology consulting work together rather than as isolated capabilities.

### Domain-Aware

> We apply specialized expertise where domain requirements matter, particularly across healthcare and clinical data.

---

# 7. ADD A "HOW WE THINK" SECTION

This would make XELARVIS feel more mature.

# **How We Think**

### Start With the Problem

Technology comes after understanding the business challenge.

### Build on Trusted Data

Reliable intelligence starts with reliable data.

### Use AI Where It Creates Value

AI should solve a meaningful problem, not simply be added because it is fashionable.

### Engineer for Production

Solutions should be scalable, secure and maintainable.

### Measure the Outcome

Success should be evaluated through measurable impact.

This can become one of your strongest brand sections.

---

# 8. RESEARCH POSITIONING

Since XELARVIS has a strong AI/research direction, mention it here—but don't turn About into Research.

## **Research Meets Engineering**

> XELARVIS explores emerging technologies in artificial intelligence, machine learning, healthcare AI and data science while focusing on their practical application.

Then:

**Explore Research & Innovation →**

This creates the connection:

```
ABOUT
 ↓
Research
 ↓
Services
 ↓
Solutions
```

---

# 9. HEALTHCARE POSITIONING

Because Healthcare & Clinical Data Science is a specialized XELARVIS capability, give it a small dedicated section.

# **Domain Expertise Where It Matters**

> Our healthcare and clinical data capabilities combine data science, statistical programming and clinical data standards to support data-driven healthcare and life-sciences workflows.

Then:

**Explore Healthcare & Clinical Data Science →**

Do not make unsupported claims such as:

> "Trusted by leading pharmaceutical companies"

unless you actually have those relationships.

---

# 10. COMPANY FACTS

This section should only contain **verified facts**.

For example:

```
Company
XELARVIS Private Limited

Established
[verified year]

Headquarters
[verified location]

Focus
AI • Data • Technology • Healthcare

Core Services
5

Solution Areas
9

Industries
[verified number]
```

### Important

Don't repeat the problem we found on the homepage:

**0+ employees**

**0+ countries**

**0+ projects**

etc.

If you don't have verified metrics, **don't show a counter**.

A clean About page with no statistics is better than a page with obviously incomplete counters.

---

# 11. LEADERSHIP / TEAM

If you have genuine leadership information, add:

# **Leadership**

Each profile:

**Name**

**Title**

Short 50–80 word biography.

Then:

- LinkedIn
- Expertise
- Research interests, where relevant

### Don't create fake team members.

If there are only one or two publicly listed leaders, that's completely fine.

You can call it:

**Leadership**

rather than pretending to have a large executive team.

---

# 12. COMPANY JOURNEY

If you have real milestones, add a timeline.

Example:

```
2024
XELARVIS founded

2025
AI & Data Services expanded

2026
Healthcare & Clinical Data Science launched

2026
European market expansion
```

Only use actual dates and events.

If you don't have enough milestones, **skip the timeline**.

Don't create a decorative timeline with meaningless dates.

---

**Explore All Solutions →**

# 15. ABOUT → CAREERS

This is important for employer branding.

At the bottom:

# **Build the Future With Us**

> We are building a team across AI, data science, engineering, consulting and healthcare technology.

**Explore Careers →**

This connects your corporate story directly to recruitment.

---

# 16. FINAL CTA

Don't use:

> "Contact us today!"

It's generic.

Use:

# **Have a Complex Technology Challenge?**

> Let's explore how AI, data and technology can create measurable value for your organization.

**Start a Conversation →**

# 18. CMS CONNECTIONS

The About page itself doesn't need to become heavily CMS-driven.

But these relationships should exist:

```
ABOUT
 │
 ├── Services →
 ├── Solutions →
 ├── Industries →
 ├── Research →
 └── Careers →
```

Your **master Technology database should not be heavily featured on About**.

Technology belongs primarily in:

**Services → Solutions → Technologies**

rather than:

**About → 25 technology logos**

That keeps the brand story clean.

---

# 19. What I would NOT put on About

Avoid:

❌ Huge technology-logo wall

❌ Generic "innovation / excellence / integrity" cards

❌ Unverified client logos

❌ Fake testimonials

❌ Fake statistics

❌ Excessive animations

❌ 1,500-word company history

❌ Repeating every service description

❌ Repeating every solution

❌ "We are the world's leading..." claims without evidence

---

# 20. Professional About-page formula

The final narrative should feel like:

> **Who we are**
>
> ↓
>
> **Why we exist**
>
> ↓
>
> **How we think**
>
> ↓
>
> **What makes us different**
>
> ↓
>
> **What we specialize in**
>
> ↓
>
> **Proof / facts**
>
> ↓
>
> **What we do**
>
> ↓
>
> **Work with us**

That's a much stronger corporate story than a traditional:

> About Us → Mission → Vision → Values → Team
